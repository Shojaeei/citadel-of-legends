Description

40 words

Citadel of Legends is a free browser castle-builder RTS. Grow a medieval citadel, balance food, taxes and popularity, then defend your walls with archers, boiling pitch and dragons across a nine-mission campaign for three asymmetric kingdoms. No install required.

100 words

Citadel of Legends: Shattered Realms is a tactical castle-builder and real-time strategy game that runs in the browser. Players raise a living medieval citadel where every peasant is a worker, a taxpayer and a mouth to feed. Rations, taxes and popularity drive immigration, while sawmills, quarries, mythril mines and brimstone rigs fuel the war machine. When enemy waves cross the river, players man watchtowers, pour boiling pitch and unleash faction dragons. Three asymmetric kingdoms — Solaris Order, Frostbound Covenant and Obsidian Scourge — each have unique champions, monuments and economies across a nine-mission campaign. Free, offline-capable, zero dependencies.

Full description

In the Shattered Realms, three powers vie for the crossings of a great river. King Arthur's Solaris Order holds the green Valley of Avalon with Sun-Blade Knights and the healing light of the Cathedral. Siegfried's Frostbound Covenant quarries basalt from the glacier and calls Valkyries from the storm. Count Vlad's Obsidian Scourge rules the blighted wastes with Bloodknights who walk unseen and cauldrons of boiling pitch.

Citadel of Legends combines a deep castle economy with real-time siege defense. Seven resources, a fragile popularity rating and an immigration system reward wise rule. Enemy armies use A* pathfinding to find the weak point in your walls — and batter it down if there is none. Fog of war, a radar minimap, box selection and four game speeds give players full tactical command, on desktop or touch devices.

Every visual is drawn live on HTML5 Canvas and every sound — from sword clashes to wolf howls on the wind — is synthesized in real time with the Web Audio API. There are no image sprites, audio files, engines or frameworks: the game loads in a heartbeat, installs as an offline app and respects player privacy with no tracking.

History

The project began as a single 1,100-line HTML prototype exploring whether a classic-style castle simulation could run with zero dependencies in a browser tab. Over five engineering milestones it grew into a modular engine: an isometric 2.5D renderer, A* pathfinding, a siege AI, faction asymmetry and ballistics, then the v1.0 work — fog of war, radar minimap, save/load with export, the nine-mission campaign, procedural soundscapes, weather, mobile touch controls and a spatial-hash simulation that keeps 250+ units at 60 FPS. The full development log is public in the project repository.

Features

  • Three asymmetric factions — unique economies, monuments, champions (Avalon Paladin, Frost Valkyrie, Vampire Bloodknight) and dragons.
  • Nine-mission narrative campaign — briefings, multi-objective missions and bonus challenges (e.g. "slay the enemy general with your dragon", "complete in under 12 minutes").
  • Deep castle economy — rations, taxes, famine, popularity-driven immigration and seven resources.
  • Real-time siege warfare — watchtowers, boiling-pitch cauldrons, parabolic catapult fire with splash damage, wall breaching and retaliation AI.
  • Fog of war & radar minimap — explore the realm; click or drag the minimap to command the camera.
  • Save anywhere — three slots, rolling auto-save and exportable .citadelsave files.
  • Procedural audio — faction soundscapes (birdsong and abbey chimes, arctic wind and campfire crackle, subterranean hum and wolf howls) with a full volume mixer.
  • Accessible & mobile-ready — high-contrast outlines, rebindable keys, reduced motion, pinch-zoom, long-press commands and a virtual joystick.
  • Zero dependencies, works offline — no engine, framework or tracking; installable as a PWA or desktop app.

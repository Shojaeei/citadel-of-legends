Asymmetric Factions

Three kingdoms that play nothing alike — not just palette swaps.

  • Solaris: holy Paladins heal the line
  • Frostbound: Valkyries strike from above
  • Obsidian: Bloodknights move unseen
Each faction has its own economic multipliers, a signature monument (Cathedral, Runestone or Blood Crucible), a unique champion and its own dragon. Solaris bakes faster and taxes harder; Frostbound quarries stone like no one else; Obsidian marches faster and brews pitch from the dark.
🍞

Deep Castle Economy

Seven resources, one fragile popularity rating.

  • Rations vs. famine, taxes vs. revolt
  • Immigration that rewards good rule
  • Timber, basalt, mythril, pitch & honor
Happy citadels attract settlers faster; starving ones bleed people. Run extortionate taxes to fund a dragon — if your people will stand for it. Monuments generate Arcane Honor, the currency of legends.
🔥

Real-Time Siege Warfare

A* pathfinding armies that breach walls, not walk through them.

  • Boiling pitch cauldrons & watchtowers
  • Parabolic catapult fire with splash damage
  • Fog of war, minimap & box selection
Enemy waves path around your defenses, batter down whatever blocks them and turn on defenders who sally out. Dragon breath leaves burning ground. Command at 0x, 1x, 2x or 5x speed.

Questions from the Court

Is it really free?

Yes. The full game — all three factions and nine missions — is free. The optional Royal Supporter Edition is a tip that unlocks cosmetic golden banners, developer commentary and an ad-free experience on web portals. It never affects balance.

What devices does it run on?

Any modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop, plus tablets and phones with touch controls (pinch to zoom, long-press to command, on-screen joystick). Desktop builds are available for Windows, macOS and Linux.

Do you collect my data?

No. Saves and settings stay in your browser on your device. There is no account, analytics or tracking in the game. If you play on an ad-supported portal, that portal's own privacy policy applies. Read our Privacy Policy.

Can I stream or make videos?

Absolutely — including monetized videos. See the Content Creator Permission in our EULA. Press and creators can grab assets from the press kit.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Once loaded, the game installs as an app (PWA) and runs without a connection. Saves can be exported as .citadelsave files and moved between devices.
